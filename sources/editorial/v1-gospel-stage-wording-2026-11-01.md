# Gospel stage wording alignment - 1 November 2026

Baseline: v0.44.15. PATCH evidence from audit https://github.com/andrewzaia/Scripture-Trails/issues/67 .

## G-01 - Nazareth rejection is not independently proved twice

Luke 4:16-30 narrates Jesus reading in a Nazareth synagogue early in Luke's Gospel. Mark 6:1-6 and Matthew 13:53-58 place hometown rejection at a later literary point. Scripture Trails retains two distinct **textual study stages**, `j020` and `j033`, as an editorial parallel-reading aid. The older `j033` title "A return to his hometown" and phrase "teaches again" could wrongly imply proof of a second independent historical visit despite the qualified study context.

Correction: neutral title and summary, with a clarification that distinct narrative positions are not proof of a separate visit. Keep source citations, event ID, research flag and existing cross-references intact.

Primary passages:
- https://bible.usccb.org/bible/luke/4
- https://bible.usccb.org/bible/mark/6
- https://bible.usccb.org/bible/matthew/13

## G-02 - Crucifixion summary reaches beyond its cited ranges

The four `j072` crucifixion readings are Matthew 27:32-56, Mark 15:21-41, Luke 23:26-49 and John 19:17-30. These cover the death and witnesses. Joseph of Arimathea's burial arrangements begin **after** these limits, Matthew 27:57, Mark 15:42, Luke 23:50 and John 19:38. The next stable event `j073` already cites precisely those burial narratives.

Correction: remove the premature "burial preparations" wording from `j072` without adding duplicate readings. Preserve the existing burial event, reference ranges, IDs and navigational order.

Primary sources:
- https://bible.usccb.org/bible/matthew/27
- https://www.esv.org/Jn19.38-42%3BMt27.57-61%3BMk15.42-47%3BLk23.50-56/
