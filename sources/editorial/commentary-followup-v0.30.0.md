## Consequential distinctions

- The Romans source offers a different intertext from the proposed Genesis echo. The supplement records that disagreement rather than treating a citation as endorsement.
- The warrior comparison retains the textual problem and the distinction between David's opponent and Elhanan's. The commentator's preferred resolution is not a completed textual investigation.
- The David notes retain the omitted Bathsheba incident and differing attribution of the census incitement. Proposed authorship, census rationale and theological reconciliation remain interpretations.
- Gospel notes preserve the original account differences and classifications, including test order, Matthew/Levi, six/about eight days, children/infants, donkey/colt, meal wording and tomb responses.
- Neither a specific healing text nor the child-welcome comparison is used to settle a medical or sacramental question.
- Acts accounts remain retellings within one literary work. A proposed distinction between hearing and understanding does not replace a wider Greek-text investigation.
