# Scripture study of Paul's first two journeys - 31 October 2026

## Scope

The 18 first-journey and 21 second-journey stages now carry 39 original Scripture-first explanatory reading notes and one or more additional canonical reading links each. Existing primary Acts passages remain the source of the narrative. This editorial work is a reader guide, not independent historical, manuscript, archaeological or specialist certification.

Source and independent comparison: [Crossway ESV first itinerary](https://www.esv.org/resources/esv-global-study-bible/chart-44-07b/) and [second itinerary](https://www.esv.org/resources/esv-global-study-bible/chart-44-08b/). Publisher maps are modern interpretive presentations, not archaeological evidence of exact travel routes or dates.

## Focused historical geography

Acts 17:14-15 recounts Paul's departure from Berea and his arrival in Athens, without documenting the exact transit route. Altena and co-authors, [*GIS as a heuristic tool to interpret ancient historiography* (Transactions in GIS, 2021)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8361720/), examine alternative reconstructions of this travel and the movements of companions. This article warrants caution in rendering an itinerary, but does not prove a single route.

## Preservation and open work

Preserved all 83 original Pauline stage IDs, existing journey selections, map pin provenance, original Acts primary links, route gaps, reader/Atlas actions and existing source licences. 44 stages in the third journey and the prisoner voyage still lack the new study-note depth. Scripture reference integrity tests, browser regression checks, release consistency validation, production deployment verification and further expert review remain separate tasks.

Source limitations: Biblical events and theological interpretations are stated as accounts in the canonical texts; links to later Pauline letters are thematic or comparative and do not alone fix historical dates. Bible translation text is not republished in the note data.
