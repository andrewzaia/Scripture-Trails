# Imported Scripture quotation classifications - first batch

Date: 2026-11-18
Release: 1.19.0

## Scope and selection

Fourteen previously unexplained source-to-destination pairs were selected from the locally hosted OpenBible.info snapshot. Every selected pair was verified against the corresponding source-book shard; none duplicates a pair in the existing 161 fully explained Atlas comparisons. These entries are narrow, first-pass quotation classifications, not fully developed exegesis and not a claim to have completed the imported corpus. The existing 198 Gospel, place, trail and Atlas assessment records remain intact.

The publisher chapter texts and editorial cross-reference notes were checked where retrieval was possible. Some direct publisher page requests returned access errors; indexed extracts were used for narrowly scoped comparisons. Each record links to its source passage and receiving passage in the USCCB Bible, without redistributing publisher translation text. The source publisher and the limits are explicit in the public register.

## Romans 9-10

- `xr001` Hosea 2:23 / Romans 9:25 - explicit quotation in Paul's argument about God's people, with Hosea's restoration context and alternate NABRE verse 2:25.
- `xr002` Isaiah 8:14 / Romans 9:33 - the stumbling-stone half of a combined Isaiah citation.
- `xr003` Isaiah 28:16 / Romans 9:33 - the foundation-stone half of the same combined citation.
- `xr004` Deuteronomy 30:12 / Romans 10:6 - adapted question about ascending to heaven, reinterpreted Christologically.
- `xr005` Deuteronomy 30:14 / Romans 10:8 - adapted near-word language concerning faith proclamation.
- `xr006` Isaiah 53:1 / Romans 10:16 - the servant poem's hearing question cited in a gospel proclamation argument.
- `xr007` Isaiah 65:1 / Romans 10:20 - citation about those who did not seek God, with original covenant context distinguished.
- `xr008` Isaiah 65:2 / Romans 10:21 - citation about divine appeal to a disobedient people; no collective blame is inferred.

## Hebrews 1

- `xr009` Deuteronomy 32:43 / Hebrews 1:6 - Greek-dependent angel-worship wording, with Psalm 97:7 as an additional possible source. The Masoretic and Greek textual traditions cannot be treated as identical.
- `xr010` 2 Samuel 7:14 / Hebrews 1:5 - Davidic father-and-son promise used in the sonship argument.
- `xr011` Psalm 104:4 / Hebrews 1:7 - Greek Septuagint wording has a different relationship between angels, winds and messengers than the Hebrew.
- `xr012` Psalm 45:6 / Hebrews 1:8 - royal throne and divine kingship language, numbered 45:7 in NABRE.
- `xr013` Psalm 102:25 / Hebrews 1:10 - the Creator's permanence quoted in the Son context, numbered 102:26 in NABRE.
- `xr014` Psalm 110:1 / Hebrews 1:13 - royal right-hand oracle applied to the Son.

## Research limits and next stages

The classifications do not establish a manuscript exemplar, settle original-language variants or imply an independent specialist reviewed them. The Hebrew-Greek divergence in Psalm 104:4 is recorded as a textual limitation. The 161 fully explained Atlas comparisons remain distinct, while 301,392 pairs lack full editorial explanations; 14 now have source-only classification, leaving 301,378 without either level. Later batches can prioritise verified New Testament quotations before attempting lower-confidence thematic matches. OpenBible votes may guide discovery but must not determine the classification.
