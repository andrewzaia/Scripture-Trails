# Journey evidence review - 31 October 2026

## Scope and evidence level

The published datasets retain 79 Gospel events and 83 Pauline stages. Their existing validation checks establish stage IDs, passage addressability, source-marker structure and chronology grouping. This targeted editorial pass compares the passages below to the current displayed claims and changes disputed geography where Scripture does not support an asserted route. It does **not** constitute a complete textual, archaeological or specialist review of all 162 entries.

Bible passages are the primary narrative references. We consulted the linked publishers' online passages and the abstract of an academic journal article. We did not reproduce licensed Bible text, read the paid article in full, collate manuscripts or consult independent reviewers. Evidence grades for unaddressed stages remain as previously recorded.

## Gospel observations

- **Mark 6:45-53 and John 6:16-21; j035/j036:** Mark distinguishes the intended Bethsaida destination from the Gennesaret landfall; John reports heading toward Capernaum. The two map notes now preserve that tension. [Publisher text](https://www.esv.org/verses/Mark%2B6%3A45%E2%80%9356/). [Olegs Andrejevs, *Mark's Framing of the Walking on the Sea Incident* (2022), journal abstract](https://doi.org/10.2143/ETL.98.2.3290792). This paper surveys interpretations of the narrative tension; its abstract cannot determine the historical course.
- **Luke 18:35, Mark 10:46 and Matthew 20:29; j058:** One account describes approach, others departure; Matthew reports two blind men while Mark names Bartimaeus. [Publisher comparison](https://www.esv.org/Lk18.35%3BMt20.29%3BMk10.46/). The map does not decide the historical number of incidents.
- **Luke 24:50 and Acts 1:12; j079:** Toward Bethany and the Mount of Olives identify a regional setting, not a surveyed ascension location. [Published comparison](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Luke+24%3A50%2CMark+11%3A1%2CActs+1%3A12&version=NIV).

## Pauline observations

- **Acts 18:22; paul-second-20:** The text names Caesarea and Antioch but not the intermediate church's city. Jerusalem is an interpretation, not a directly stated name. [Publisher text](https://www.esv.org/verses/Acts%2018%3A18%E2%80%9322/).
- **Acts 20:13-17; paul-third-10/12/13:** Paul went by land to Assos; the ship went opposite Chios, touched at Samos, and reached Miletus. The elders travelled from Ephesus to him. [Publisher text](https://www.esv.org/verses/Acts%2B20%3A13%E2%80%9338/).
- **Acts 27:8-16; paul-rome-08/09:** Fair Havens was reached, Phoenix was only proposed, and the crew passed in the lee of Cauda amid storm conditions. [Publisher text](https://www.esv.org/verses/Acts%2027/).
- **Acts 27:17-28:1; paul-rome-10/11/12/13:** Storm drift, soundings and an unidentified bay do not yield coordinates. Acts identifies Malta as the island only after survival. [Publisher text](https://www.esv.org/verses/Acts%2B27%E2%80%9328%3A16/).
