# Twenty connection assessments - 29 October 2026

## Scope and method

Batch `connections-20-03` adds twenty previously unassessed pairs. The register now contains 68 of 115 explained connections; 47 retain their prior review labels. Thirty-six assessed connections have a named scholarly commentary or limited publisher-summary source. These counts are editorial coverage measures, not peer-review or historical-certification measures.

Every new record has two checked primary passage views and one named readable commentary, with a locator and a specific statement of what that source supports. Fifteen commentaries were read at the recorded scope. The selection covers creation, shepherd imagery, renewal, Law, mission and salvation. All nineteen thematic labels remain thematic; ce038 retains its editorial literary-echo label. No direct-quotation label was added.

The Bible is the authoritative foundation of the site. The paired comparison is the project's editorial judgment; a cited commentator is credited only for the claim stated in the citation. These Christian lectionary and evangelical commentaries have explicit confessional settings. Their cited books, textual apparatuses and wider historical claims were not independently examined. Independent specialist review remains pending.

## Access and rights

USCCB chapter views supplied the checked ranges where readable. Crossway's ESV chapter views supplied Genesis 1, Psalms 23, 46 and 51, Isaiah 9 and 40, Luke 15 and Romans 5 and 8. Several ESV views contain neighbouring chapters; only the declared ranges underpin these assessments. USCCB retrieval was unavailable for Genesis 1, Isaiah 40, Luke 15 and Romans 5 and 8. ESV numbering was used for the selected Psalms and Isaiah 9 to keep the app's English references explicit. No translation-equivalence or manuscript audit is claimed.

No publisher passage or commentary text is retained in the repository. The site stores original assessment prose, bibliographic metadata, claim locators and links. Public accessibility was not treated as redistribution permission. The JSON register is the authoritative source; `node scripts/import-citation-review.mjs` regenerates its browser bundle.

## Retained limits

- Genesis/Revelation is a broad creation comparison; Isaiah 65:17 is the more immediate verbal background identified for Revelation 21:1. Tree-of-life imagery also has an Ezekiel 47 background. No modern Eden geography or scientific chronology is proposed.
- Psalm 23, Ezekiel 34, Luke 15 and John 10 retain distinct voices and settings. Care and justice, welcoming sinners, and self-giving shepherding are related without becoming the same episode or a verbatim quotation.
- Isaiah 9:6 is not explicitly quoted by Luke 2. Royal setting, title syntax and Hebrew/Greek differences remain distinct from later Christian thematic reception.
- Matthew 5 and Romans 3 do not become identical arguments about Torah. Matthew 28 and Acts 1 retain their Galilee and Jerusalem/ascension settings. No harmonised speech or mission-based permission for domination is inferred.
- Prayer, renewed strength and cosmic hope are not presented as exemption from distress, effortless circumstances or a fixed timetable. John 1 and Philippians 2 retain their revelatory and ethical settings.
