# Gospel and historical-city evidence cross-check
Reviewed 1 November 2026. Publication candidate: v0.44.12.

The Gospel journey audit has historically lagged behind the archaeological and textual references in the site's detailed historical-city studies.

**Bethsaida:** Luke and other Gospel references name Bethsaida but cannot fix a modern site. Both the et-Tell team (https://ngsba.org/excavations/bethsaida/) and el-Araj team (https://www.bethsaida-julias.com/season8report and https://www.bethsaida-julias.com/updates) document material finds and advance competing identifications. The 2025 el-Araj field reports refer to Roman material below Byzantine floors. This does not adjudicate the Gospel site or verify a particular narrated event.

**Emmaus:** Luke 24:13 is commonly read as 60 stadia; some witnesses read 160. A separate ancient Josephus Emmaus/Colonia report has its own 30-versus-60-stadia variant. These textual transmissions cannot be merged into one certainty. Sources: https://www.biblegateway.com/resources/encyclopedia-of-the-bible/Emmaus and https://bible.org/seriespage/luke-24.

The release adds sources already recorded in the historical-city evidence registry, titles their links and verifies cross-study consistency and browser display. Both site-identification questions remain **open**, and no independent historical, textual or archaeological certification is claimed. Private publisher agreements, full copyrighted articles and licensed Bible text are not copied.
