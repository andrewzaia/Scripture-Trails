# Gospel geography source audit - 31 October 2026

## Scope

Reviewed the source classification and statement of uncertainty on all 35 Gospel place dossiers. Twenty-three existing dossiers cite place-related external literature; twelve are deliberately broad geographical placeholders, where inventing an archaeological site citation would be misleading. A dated, individually readable claim-to-source-and-limits panel was added to five places with contested identifications or commemorative traditions: Bethsaida, Emmaus, Magadan, Sychar and Gethsemane.

**Scope boundary:** the five new comparisons are based on accessible abstracts, publishers' bibliographic pages, existing excavation reports and previously linked research, not independent specialist archaeological validation of all 35 dossier claims. The user is not being asked to certify these claims manually. The content-audit and critical-claims v1.0 criteria must remain open.

## Research ledger

- **Bethsaida:** Existing [el-Araj 2024 excavation field report](https://www.bethsaida-julias.com/season8report), supplemented by Mordechai Aviam and R. Steven Notley, *In Search of the City of the Apostles*, [Novum Testamentum 63(2), 143-158 (2021)](https://doi.org/10.1163/15685365-12341710), abstract publicly indexed; and Rami Arav, *Searching for Bethsaida: The Case for et-Tell*, [University of Haifa research record, Biblical Archaeology Review 46(2) (2020)](https://cris.haifa.ac.il/en/publications/searching-for-bethsaida-the-case-for-et-tell/). These represent competing positions, not a proven site or evidence of a specific Gospel scene. Neither complete paper was accessed.
- **Emmaus:** Existing [IAA Moza excavation report](https://hadashot.iaa.org.il/Report_Detail_Eng.aspx?id=25953) supports regional Roman-era context, while B. P. Robinson, *The Place of the Emmaus Story in Luke-Acts*, [New Testament Studies 30(4), 481-497 (1984)](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0028688500013199), publisher extract explicitly treats the modern location as uncertain. The Luke 24:13 distance has manuscript variants (commonly 60 stadia with a minority 160-stadia tradition), but neither the full apparatus nor all site reports were independently collated in this pass.
- **Magadan, Dalmanutha and Magdala:** Existing [IAA archaeological report at Magdala](https://publications.iaa.org.il/atiqot/vol90/iss1/7/) shows archaeological occupation. Joan E. Taylor, *Missing Magdala and the Name of Mary 'Magdalene'*, [Palestine Exploration Quarterly 146(3), 205-223 (2014)](https://doi.org/10.1179/0031032814Z.000000000110), King's College London institutional abstract checked. Its proposed Magadan/Magdala connection is an interpretative position, not a consensus identification of Matthew and Mark's landing point.
- **Sychar:** Existing [Radiocarbon site dating study on Jacob's Well](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/radiocarbon/article/abs/new-chronological-information-from-radiocarbon-dating-of-human-remains-at-jacobs-well-nablus-palestine/2B98AB26475BF63019F4C2C5BAF7569D) concerns human remains associated with the modern well. Reinhard Pummer, *Shechem and the Samaritans*, [Ancient Near Eastern Studies 59, 171-189 (2022)](https://doi.org/10.2143/ANES.59.0.3291193), publisher abstract checked. Neither settles Sychar's footprint or identifies the people in John 4.
- **Gethsemane:** Previously linked [Bernabei, olive-tree dating research](https://iris.cnr.it/handle/20.500.14243/287216) and the [IAA Second Temple remains announcement](https://www.friendsofiaa.org/news/2020/12/29/second-temple-period-remains-unearthed-at-jerusalems-gethsemane-the-first-remains-from-the-era-that-jesus-visited-the-site-according-to-christian-belief) provide distinct contexts for material remains and later site tradition. Living tree age and area archaeology cannot verify the exact garden plot of prayer and arrest. The excavation announcement is not a final stratigraphic report.

## Methodology and preservation

No artefact or published article body has been reproduced in the website. Original 79 Gospel stage IDs, all 35 places, bibliography history, site coordinates, notebook continuity, reader translations, deep links, prior source audit entries and site status labels remain intact. Five source-linked site comparisons have dated `identificationReview` records; source counts and source-check ledgers are consistent. Region-only marker gaps are not falsely marked as defective source coverage.

Required checks before publishing: semantic-version release script, build and Node tests, full browser regression, pull-request-wide release gate, Cloudflare deployment matching the merged SHA, live footer and high-risk site-content smoke tests.
