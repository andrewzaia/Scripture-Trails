# v1.0 flagship content audit - five historical city studies

Review date: 2026-10-31
Prepared website release: v0.36.0
Audit classification: AI-assisted scoped biblical-text and published-source review. Not external peer review.

## Scope and method

This first flagship-content batch covers five existing historical city dossiers: Jerusalem, Nazareth, Capernaum, Bethlehem and Jericho. The underlying city and Scripture indexes and modern-photo licence records are retained. Fifteen new substantial study passages organise each city's narrative into three levels: what the Bible actually describes, what excavated material can establish, and what remains interpretively unsettled. Every layer has clickable passages, an explicit evidence boundary and source links for material history.

This work does not certify the historical details of all 79 Jesus events, 83 Pauline events, 173 named people or six major guided study journals. The v1.0 flagship-content launch gate stays OPEN until those areas are reviewed. All claims of independent scholarly or physical-device testing remain expressly unverified where inaccessible.

### Primary archaeology and heritage references checked

- Yardenna Alexandre, **The Settlement History of Nazareth in the Iron Age and Early Roman Period**, 'Atiqot 98 (2020), pp. 25-92, https://publications.iaa.org.il/atiqot/vol98/iss1/3/ . Abstract identifies three excavation strata.
- Ken Dark, **Early Roman-period Nazareth and the Sisters of Nazareth convent**, The Antiquaries Journal 92 (2012), https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/antiquaries-journal/article/abs/early-romanperiod-nazareth-and-the-sisters-of-nazareth-convent/CC6D566B2F905B663540434FC495A37B . Accessible abstract describes a partially rock-cut structure and tentative domestic interpretation.
- Johanna Regev et al., **Radiocarbon chronology of Iron Age Jerusalem reveals calibration offsets and architectural developments**, PNAS (2024), https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11087761/ . Article describes 103 samples from five archaeological excavation areas, distinct from 100 additional tree rings used to test calibration.
- Bornblum Eretz Israel Synagogues Website, **Capernaum**, https://synagogues.kinneret.ac.il/synagogues/capernaum/ . Inventory describes later limestone synagogue over basalt and dating disagreement.
- UNESCO, **Birthplace of Jesus: Church of the Nativity and the Pilgrimage Route, Bethlehem**, https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1433 . Material monuments and dates document Christian commemoration, not proof of the exact birth event.
- UNESCO, **Ancient Jericho/Tell es-Sultan**, https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1687 . Bronze and Neolithic phases, a different archaeological period from the Gospels.
- UNESCO, **Archaeological Palaces of Tulul Abu el-'Alayiq**, https://whc.unesco.org/en/tentativelists/6947/ . Tentative State Party submission dated 13 January 2026; not inscribed.
- Hendrik J. Bruins and Johannes van der Plicht, **Tell es-Sultan (Jericho): Radiocarbon Results of Short-Lived Cereal and Multiyear Charcoal Samples from the End of the Middle Bronze Age**, Radiocarbon 37 (1995), https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/radiocarbon/article/tell-essultan-jericho-radiocarbon-results-of-shortlived-cereal-and-multiyear-charcoal-samples-from-the-end-of-the-middle-bronze-age/49BF732173E7890A2B0EC1B21CB6A817 . Eighteen samples; tentative late seventeenth/sixteenth century BCE calibration, not a securely identified biblical conquest date.

## Limitations and next actions

The Bible is primary for what its authors narrate. Historical and scientific material is contextual support, not a substitute for the passages or an automatic proof of miracle reports. A small excavated area cannot represent an entire city, and a shrine's long-term veneration is not proof that its exact location was originally known. These distinctions remain visible in the newly added material.

Next audit the **20 person study trails**, **six major reading journals**, **Jesus and Pauline journeys**, and cross-navigation. Address material inaccuracies before declaring the flagship-content v1.0 criterion met. A separate copyright and API-licence audit also remains open.
