## Scope

Batch `connections-final-07` completes dated editorial assessment coverage for the existing 115 explained pairs. It adds seven records with paired passage contexts, three named commentary works by three authors, and explicit limitations. There are now 152 total assessment records and 301 source entries. The three commentaries come from one evangelical collection; the selection is not a broad review of competing scholarship.

## Consequential distinctions

- Ahaz: keep Kings' report of the Assyrian response distinct from Chronicles' lack-of-help judgment and its Oded episode. Military and captive totals and campaign reconstruction are not independently confirmed.
- Manasseh: retain the repentance episode absent from Kings alongside Kings' innocent-blood and judgment emphasis. The comparison does not independently corroborate captivity or recover the text of a prayer.
- Josiah's scroll: preserve Huldah, delegation-name differences and the debated identification and extent of the book. Its discovery does not itself date the Torah's composition. The burial-in-peace promise is not silently reconciled with the later death narrative.
- Josiah's death: retain Kings' dead return from Megiddo and Chronicles' wounded return before death, divine-warning explanation and lament. No final itinerary or political motive is established.
- Zedekiah: literary overlap does not establish independent witnesses, compiler identity or accession chronology. Hamutal's father's name alone does not identify him with the prophet.
- Siege: explicitly retain the seventh/tenth-day difference at Kings 25:8 and Jeremiah 52:12 and the fuller captivity and deportation details. No multi-day harmonisation or absolute chronology is established.
- Returnees: preserve the shared 42,360 total and the differing Arah figures, names, singers and gifts. Direction of dependence, common-source and registration or transmission explanations remain open. No modern ethnic hierarchy follows.
