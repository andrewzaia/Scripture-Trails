# Scripture Trails - owner scholarly and editorial acceptance for v1.0

Date: 1 November 2026 (Australia/Sydney)

## Decision and attribution

The Scripture Trails project owner explicitly stated in the project discussion on this date that they had personally checked **all published scholarly and editorial content** and accepted its quality. They also expressly rejected a mandatory second scholarly approval from a professor or other external specialist and committed to evaluating specific future scholarly questions themselves when needed.

This is an actual **owner-declared scholarly and editorial signoff**. It is not independently witnessed peer review, not a statement that every past interpretation enjoys academic consensus, not a new model-produced line-by-line verification, and not a promise that subsequent archaeology or research can never warrant an update.

For the v1 roadmap this is sufficient owner approval of the **critical-claims** and **content-audit** criteria. Source citations and qualified arguments remain part of the publication, and future substantive defects require normal versioned correction.

## Source and navigation evidence available to the owner

The published materials include six guided journals, twenty people trails with 88 reading stages, 79 Gospel narrative stages, 83 Pauline stages, historical city and biblical geography studies, and 161 explained Atlas connection pairs. Supporting technical and editorial records include:

- `public/data/citation-review.json`, with dated Atlas reference-to-claim assessments.
- `public/data/jesus-audit.json`, retaining disputed geographical identifications, qualifications and historical limitations.
- `sources/editorial/v1-flagship-content-audit-inventory-2026-10-31.md`, with initial reading-address screening, subsequent documented findings and corrections.
- `sources/editorial/v1-people-ci-optimisation-2026-11-01.md` and related Gospel reading corrections, including expanded passages for Moses, Solomon and the Gospel stages.
- GitHub Issue #67, with documented serial source-context screenings across the Gospel and Pauline journey stages, geography dossiers and people trails.
- `sources/operations/v1-production-acceptance-v0.44.19-2026-11-01.md`, containing actual real-browser reader and navigation evidence.

The source counts are aids to audit completeness, not an external scholarly certificate. Personal content review is represented **only** as the owner's stated acceptance; the assistant did not observe that private review.

## Exclusions

Editorial acceptance does **not** establish API.Bible or Crossway licence entitlement, publisher-side FUMS accounting, real NVDA/JAWS/VoiceOver use, physical devices and true browser zoom, actual production rollback or final v1.0 launch authorisation. Those are separate roadmap gates.

**Recorded result:** owner scholarly and editorial gates accepted on 2026-11-01, without requiring external professor approval.
