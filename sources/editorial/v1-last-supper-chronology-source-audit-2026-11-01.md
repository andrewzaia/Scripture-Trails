# Last Supper Passover chronology: source-specific editorial review
Date: 1 November 2026
Scope: Scripture Trails event j069 and audit issue last-supper-calendar.

## Primary biblical accounts
Mark 14:12-26, Matthew 26:17-30 and Luke 22:7-38 describe preparing for Passover and sharing a final meal. John 13:1 introduces a supper in a different Passover framing, while John 18:28 and 19:14 speak of the festival and preparation in the Passion sequence. 1 Corinthians 11:23-26 transmits the supper tradition but does not independently establish a civil calendar date.

**Editorial conclusion:** Scripture Trails should name these primary textual differences, present the Gospel comparison as textual rather than a measured chronology, and avoid asserting that the site, precise room, Nisan date or a single modern reconstructed calendar has been proved. This does not deny any account's theological claim; it separates what the texts say from historical harmonisations.

## Named secondary research and limits

1. Joel Marcus, "Passover and Last Supper Revisited", New Testament Studies 59(3), 2013, pp. 303-324, https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/new-testament-studies/article/passover-and-last-supper-revisited/B7955FEA669F7DD71A36F008A2FE5144 . His argument favours the pre-Passover timing in John but identifies important Passover ritual and interpretive characteristics in the meal tradition. This is an author's interpretation, not a universally demonstrated date.
2. "Dating the Death of Jesus: Memory and the Religious Imagination", New Testament Studies, https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/new-testament-studies/article/dating-the-death-of-jesus-memory-and-the-religious-imagination/0458FC0CA55BEF810BCC411AC2765860 . The accessible scholarly analysis states the canonical chronological tension and discusses harmonisation via alternative calendars as one proposal. It does not establish a single uncontested resolution.
3. John P. Joy, "Ratzinger and Aquinas on the Dating of the Last Supper: In Defense of the Synoptic Chronology", New Blackfriars, https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/new-blackfriars/article/abs/ratzinger-and-aquinas-on-the-dating-of-the-last-supper-in-defense-of-the-synoptic-chronology/CDABC5A633A6F3DCB86C3174F2AB2964 . This explicitly argues for a harmonisation defending the Synoptic chronology, demonstrating that the interpretations remain contested, not that Aquinas' solution is independently proved.
