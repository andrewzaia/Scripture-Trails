ABOUT SCRIPTURE TRAILS

Discover with discernment.

Designed for thoughtful Bible study, where Scripture is kept distinct from commentary.

A study tool, not a replacement for Scripture.

Scripture Trails lets you read an ESV passage, follow verse references and explore editorially selected connections. The first release includes a curated starter network; full-canon reference coverage can be imported from an openly licensed dataset.

How connections are labelled

Quotation: a New Testament author quotes or cites another passage. Parallel: similar accounts or teaching. Echo: shared imagery or wording. Theme: a human-curated thematic association. Additional imported links are labelled as dataset references, not automatically classified as quotation or prophecy.

History and interpretation

The Bible is the primary text for biblical claims. Historical background, authorship, dating and interpretative explanations should separately identify their evidence and uncertainty. Connected verses do not automatically establish a particular theological reading.

Data and privacy

Saved passages and notes are kept in your browser's local storage; there is no account or cloud sync. ESV text is requested from Crossway through a server-side API proxy where enabled, never embedded in this application's downloadable source.

Source and licence information

ESV: Official English Standard Version text supplied by Crossway's API when the site operator has obtained a valid key. The ESV is copyrighted and is not included in the repository.

Cross-references: The curated starter links are editorial. The optional import script supports the CC BY 4.0 cross-reference dataset from OpenBible.info, primarily based on the public-domain Treasury of Scripture Knowledge. Attribution must be retained after import.
